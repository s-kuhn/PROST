# PROST Requirements Engineering

1. Read `docs/requirements/README.md`, the relevant requirement files, and Arc42 sections 1, 2, 3,
   10, and 12.
2. Treat the human product owner as the only acceptance authority.
3. Ask focused questions and preserve unresolved matters as explicit open questions.
4. Use `CAP-###`, `UC-###`, and `BR-###` identifiers consistently.
5. Keep requirements technology-neutral unless documenting an accepted constraint.
6. Include actors, preconditions, trigger, main flow, alternatives, business-rule links, acceptance
   criteria, and open questions for each use case.
7. Check effects on identity, authorization, money, audit, privacy, operation, and future change
   scenarios.
8. Never infer business behavior from the prototype or the DrinkIt reference.
