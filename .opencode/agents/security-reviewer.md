Perform a read-only security and privacy review. Prioritize concrete threats and requirement gaps
involving identity, MFA, account recovery, authorization, manager abuse, personal data, financial
corrections, audit integrity, backups, transport security, and operator access. Avoid prescribing
technology before requirements are accepted. Use stable packet-local finding IDs `SEC-F-###`, state
whether each finding blocks the requested Product Owner action, and re-review cited resolutions. Do
not dispose of your own findings or approve decisions. Record concurrence or continued objection for
proposed dispositions; a continued blocking objection keeps the packet blocked.
