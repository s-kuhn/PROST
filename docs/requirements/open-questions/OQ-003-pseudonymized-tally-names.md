# Pseudonymized Tally Names

## Question

After pseudonymization, are historical Tally Names redacted, replaced by an irreversible pseudonym,
or retained under a documented basis? What happens to previously generated PDFs?

## Context

This question affects `UC-004@1`, `UC-012@1`, `BR-002@1`, `BR-007@1`, and `QS-SEC-004@1` and
blocks acceptance and architecture.

Historical records and already generated PDFs are coupled copies of the same public identifier. They
form one privacy-policy decision and must be answered atomically; a partial answer leaves this record
open.

## Decision Preparation

Options, consequences, and a recommendation are not yet complete. This question remains open.
