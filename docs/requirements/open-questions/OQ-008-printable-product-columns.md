# Printable Product Columns

## Question

What minimum Product-column width and Product-name constraints make an A3 Tally legible?

## Product Owner Answer

Minimum 17 mm columns, complete names, and 10 pt minimum headings.

## Incorporation

The resolution is bound to `BR-006@1`, `UC-004@1`, and `QS-USA-004@1`.
