# Stakeholders

| Stakeholder                  | Interests And Expectations                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
|------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Consumer                     | Easy registration, recognizable Tally Name, private and accurate balance, understandable itemized history, usable mobile access, and fair corrections.                                                                       |
| Organization Manager         | Fast PDF preparation, reliable transcription, correction of human errors, debt visibility, manageable onboarding, and low operational overhead.                                                                              |
| Treasurer                    | MFA-protected access to anonymized financial and consumption aggregates without personal account details.                                                                                                                    |
| Deployment Operator          | Reproducible installation, provider-neutral integration, actionable diagnostics, secure public deployment, backup, restore, and upgrade guidance. The operator is technically privileged and may access infrastructure data. |
| Product Owner                | Requirement authority, scope control, terminology consistency, architecture acceptance, and sustainable evolution.                                                                                                           |
| Maintainer                   | Traceable decisions, automated verification, understandable module boundaries, and safe releases.                                                                                                                            |
| Potential Pilot Organization | Validation of the paper workflow, settlement usability, identity onboarding, and self-hosting assumptions. Commitment is not yet confirmed.                                                                                  |

One person may hold Consumer, Organization Manager, and Treasurer roles simultaneously. Effective
permissions are the union of assigned roles; privileged actions remain auditable.
